1. About Farther

Farther is a walking analytics application for iPhone. It reads step count data from Apple Health and uses that data to calculate a Lifestyle Score, which is an exponentially weighted moving average of daily step activity computed over a certain period. This score is visualised through a Band Graph, a daily activity Cone, and a Day Heatmap.

The application is designed to give users a long-term, stable picture of how they move through life, without the pressure of daily targets or streaks.

Farther is a personal wellness tool. It is not a medical device, a clinical application, or a substitute for professional medical advice.
